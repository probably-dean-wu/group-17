Use your live location

This local page shares your device location with the AccessGraph desktop app after you allow it in your browser. Keep this page open while sharing.

Your location is kept in memory. Displaying a Google map or opening a Google Maps journey also sends the requested coordinates to Google. Your browser or operating system may use its own location services.

Choose Start sharing location, then allow location access.

You can stop sharing here or in AccessGraph. If you deny permission, you can still enter a destination address and open the journey in Google Maps.
